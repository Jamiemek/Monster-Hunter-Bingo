A live room lets your clicks update both players' screens. It runs on Archipelago, a free multiplayer service, and takes about a minute to set up. Leave the boxes blank to run the race on this screen only.

  1. Open the ChecksFinder options page on Archipelago. The race doesn't use that game; it just gives the room something to host.
  2. Type a player name at the top and click Generate Game at the bottom. Leave every other option as it is.
  3. On the page that opens, click Create Room.
  4. The room page shows an address like archipelago.gg:38281 and your player name. Copy both into the boxes below.
  5. Rooms go to sleep after two hours without use. Opening the room page again wakes it; you can find your rooms under User Content.

Stuck? See the Archipelago setup guide.
